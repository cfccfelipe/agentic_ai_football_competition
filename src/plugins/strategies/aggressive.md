---
name: aggressive
type: strategy
formation: 1-0-3
description: >
  High, possession-into-attack pressure — not reckless. Every player pushes forward, wins
  the ball back purposefully high up the pitch, builds quickly and takes ON-target shots
  (not speculative blasts). The GK plays as a sweeper-keeper; defenders join attacks.
  Choose this when chasing the game (losing by any margin) OR when the score is level and
  we want to go and win it — not only when losing. gameTime is in SECONDS and matches can
  run long, so you do not have to wait for the clock to run down to attack with intent.
