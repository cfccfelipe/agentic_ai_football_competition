---
name: aggressive
type: strategy
formation: 1-1-2
description: >
  High, possession-into-attack pressure — not reckless, and NOT the default. One defender,
  one midfield link, two forwards (1-1-2): still keeps a back and a mid for structure while
  committing two players high. Every player pushes forward, wins the ball back purposefully
  high, builds quickly and takes ON-target shots (not speculative blasts); the GK plays as a
  controlled sweeper-keeper inside its own third. Choose this ONLY when genuinely chasing —
  losing by any margin, OR level and DELIBERATELY pushing to win it. Do NOT pick aggressive
  at kickoff 0-0 by default: start with balanced and only escalate here when the scoreline
  says you must. gameTime is in SECONDS and matches run long, so ~120s is EARLY — you do not
  need to wait for the clock to attack, but neither does an early clock justify going
  forward-heavy while level.
