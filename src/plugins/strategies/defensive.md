formation: 3-0-1
description: >
  Ultra-defensive block — reserved ONLY for protecting a CLEAR lead LATE in the match.
  Every player sits deep and protects the goal; forwards track back, the GK never leaves
  its line, and even here we keep the ball rather than gifting it back. gameTime is in
  SECONDS and matches can run long, so an early or low gameTime (for example around 120
  seconds) is NOT late — do NOT drop into this on a slim or early lead. If the lead is not
  clear or it is not late, choose balanced or aggressive instead.
