---
name: defensive
type: strategy
formation: 2-1-1
description: >
  A solid block to PROTECT A LEAD — reserved ONLY for guarding a CLEAR lead LATE in the
  match. Shape is 2-1-1 (two real defenders, one midfield screen, one outlet forward): a
  consistent two-defender base with the whole team compact and dropping to defend. We chose
  2-1-1 over a 3-0-1 park-the-bus so the team always keeps >=2 defenders AND a midfield
  screen without abandoning the press entirely; even here we keep the ball rather than
  gifting it back. gameTime is in SECONDS and matches run long, so an early or low gameTime
  (for example around 120 seconds) is NOT late — do NOT drop into this on a slim or early
  lead. If the lead is not clear or it is not late, choose balanced (or aggressive if
  chasing) instead.
