formation: 1-2-1
description: >
  The confident DEFAULT. Possession-based football with structure — build out through the
  midfield, push the shape up the pitch, and attack with numbers rather than sitting deep.
  Each player holds its natural role (GK guards and distributes to keep the ball, defenders
  defend and build out, midfielders link and progress play, forwards get on-target shots).
  Choose this whenever no CLEAR late-lead-protection condition holds — the game is level,
  or you lead or trail by a slim margin, with time still left. gameTime is in SECONDS and
  matches can run long, so an early or low gameTime is NOT a reason to sit back. Stay
  ambitious here; defensive is only for protecting a clear lead late.
