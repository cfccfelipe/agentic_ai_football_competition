name: balanced
type: strategy
formation: 2-1-1
description: >
  The confident DEFAULT and the shape to START with. Two real defenders, one midfield
  link, one forward (2-1-1) — possession-based football with structure: build out through
  the midfield, push the shape up, and attack with numbers rather than sitting deep. GK
  guards and distributes to keep the ball; the back two defend and build out; the mid links
  and progresses; the forward gets on-target shots. Choose this whenever no CLEAR
  late-lead-protection condition holds — the game is level, or you lead or trail by a slim
  margin, with time still left. gameTime is in SECONDS and matches run long, so an early or
  low gameTime (for example around 120 seconds) is NOT a reason to sit back AND NOT a reason
  to go all-out: at kickoff 0-0 START HERE, structured. Stay ambitious; aggressive is only
  for genuinely chasing and defensive only for protecting a clear lead late.
